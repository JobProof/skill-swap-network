Get Started

How It Works

Step 1

Post a Need

Describe the task you need help with and estimate the hours — a leaky faucet, a garden bed, a laptop tune-up.

Step 2

Offer a Skill

List what you can do for others. Match with someone nearby who needs what you offer.

Step 3

Swap & Confirm

Agree on a swap, track hours traded, upload proof photos, and confirm when both sides are done.
